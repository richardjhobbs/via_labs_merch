# CLAUDE.md — via_labs_merch

VIA Merch, the live product at merch.getvia.xyz: agents for merchandising teams in buying offices, brands, factories and trading companies. Separate from the getvia.xyz site (repo `via_labs`, which keeps the `/merch` demo) and the VIA App (repo `via-app`).

## Layout

- `web/` Next.js 15 (App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind v4, next-intl). `src/app/(public)`: landing, login, invite. `src/app/app/[tenant]`: the workspace. `src/app/admin`: the VIA console. `src/app/api/{jobs,webhooks,mcp}`.
- `supabase/migrations/` SQL applied to the shared Supabase project **VIA Labs Projects** (`jiimjfqkeuvveculyill`). All Merch objects live in schema **`merch`**; never create tables in `public` (other VIA Labs apps own it). Apply with the Supabase MCP `apply_migration` and commit the file.
- Every table has `tenant_id` and RLS. Reads: members of the tenant and platform admins. The ledger is append-only and written only through `merch.ledger_append()`.

## Rules

- Two tracks (founder, 9 Nov 2026; `docs/development-merchandising.md`, migration 0016, `lib/tracks.ts`): `production` is everything from the PO on, `development` is briefs, tech packs, materials and sample rounds before a PO exists. Separation is at the level of streams, gates (`gates` keyed by tenant, track, stream), queue, briefing, agent prompt and navigation; parties, connectors, storage and the ledger are shared, and `ledger_append` takes `p_track` and `p_style`. The company switch is `tenants.settings.tracks.development` (VIA console, "Tracks" card; switching on gives owners and managers the track, everyone else is ticked per person in the members table); a person's tracks are `memberships.tracks`; the track on screen is the `merch_track_<slug>` cookie set by the Develop/Produce switch (`components/track-switch.tsx`), read by `trackFor()`; people with one track never see the switch. The anchor of development is the style (`merch.styles`, one per code, customer and season; `orders.style_id` for later handover), and every artefact is a `merch.records` row (`lib/records.ts`): the original in the private bucket `merch-development` (30 MB, uploaded straight from the browser by signed link, served by `/api/records/file` after RLS), the extraction in `parsed`, final only when a person confirms it (`confirmed_at`, next `version`, `supersedes_id`); the document is data, never instructions. Sample approvals and rejections are always a person's action: the agent has no tool that writes a decision, a tech pack version or a style status, and the development gates for "Sample comments and approvals" and "Costing and quotes" are capped at L1. Inbound (`inbound.ts`) picks the track: a matched PO is production, a matched style code is development, else the model's reading, then `contacts.track_default`, then production; a person can move a conversation between tracks on the thread page (ledger row). Stage A (styles, records, tech pack intake, diff, translation, per-track workspace and agent) and Stage B (migration 0020, `lib/samples.ts`, `lib/dev-path.ts`, `components/dev-cards.tsx`, `app/[tenant]/styles/[id]/sample-actions.ts`, the Samples and Materials pages) are built: `merch.samples` (one row per round, `order_id` from PP on; the decision and who made it, with `decision_source` naming the customer when they decided, written only by `reviewSample` after a role check), `merch.measurements` (points of measure against the spec of `against_record_id`, pass or fail from the tolerance), `merch.materials` (BOM lines taken from the confirmed tech pack with sourcing status and supplier), `merch.dev_milestones` (the schedule, keyed from `DEFAULT_STEPS` or a `schedule_templates` row, counted back from `styles.target_delivery`, forecast by `computeDevPath` with the same slip rule as production) and `merch.style_links`. The agent reads all of it (`get_samples`, `get_materials`, `get_schedule`) and may record a sent, received or due date (`record_sample_event`), never a decision. Silence alerts cover a round due or overdue with nothing from its maker (`alerts.track`, `style_id`, `sample_id`; Chase runs the development agent). Tech packs are read after the upload is acknowledged (`records.read_status`, `readRecordJob` in `next/server after()`), never inside the request. Stage C, the handover (`app/[tenant]/styles/[id]/handover-actions.ts`, `components/handover-card.tsx`, `components/dev-history.tsx`): a person hands a style over from the style file as a new PO or an existing PO with no style, or links a PO that names a style code from the order page; `carryOver()` sets `orders.style_id` and `orders.meta.handover` (tech pack version, by, when, fabric lead), seeds the critical path from the ETD with the fabric milestone no earlier than the approved fabric's lead time, sets the mill from the one approved fabric, moves PP, TOP and shipment rounds to the order (an approved PP sets the PP milestone's actual date), marks the style `handed_over`, and writes a ledger row on each track with stream `Handover`. Never automatic. The order page shows the development behind the PO (spec version, rounds, materials) and later tech pack versions as a warning; `get_order` carries `development` for the agent. A spec change after handover is an Amendment on the PO, not a new tech pack version. Stage D, PLM routes (migration 0022): readable mail attachments (PDF, spreadsheet, image, up to 20 MB) from known senders are stored by the Gmail and Outlook connectors in the private bucket `merch-attachments` against the message (`lib/connectors/attachments.ts`, served by `/api/attachments/file`); when the inbound pipeline files a development message on a style, each attachment becomes a `records` row (source `email`, or `plm` when the sender is a PLM system from `lib/connectors/plm.ts`, which counts as a known sender once the company runs development, with the party taken from the style's customer), and a person can file an unmatched attachment on a style from the thread page. A record a job inserts with `read_status` pending is read by `/api/jobs/read-record` (same `x-merch-jobs` secret), called by the trigger `records_read_notify` through pg_net; browser uploads keep using `after()`. Delogue PLM (`lib/connectors/delogue.ts`, channel kind `plm`, `config.connector` 'delogue', personal API key sealed in `auth_enc`, read only, pilot): the drain syncs one connection per pass at most hourly, styles into `styles` (`plm_ref` delogue:style:id), readable style files into records (source `plm`, `source_ref` delogue:file:id, read in the background), sample requests into `samples` (`plm_ref`); decisions are never taken from the PLM and nothing is written back. Untested against a live Delogue account.
- Read `docs/merchandiser-records.md` (founder, 29 Oct 2026): manual merchandiser work (samples, measurements, spec adherence, inspections, third-party inputs) is a first-class, versioned, ledger-backed record, linked to PO and milestone, with any input route. Design samples, QC, documents and ledger features against it.
- Sign-in is a code or magic link on every visit; sessions are browser-session cookies and end after 2 hours idle (`src/middleware.ts`, `merch.sessions`). Do not add "remember me".
- Nothing the agent drafts is sent without the stream's gate allowing it; price, claims and order confirmation are capped at L1.
- Jobs run from `/api/jobs/drain`, called every minute by `merch.tick()` (pg_cron + pg_net) with the `x-merch-jobs` secret. Keep each drain under 55 s.
- UI strings go through `messages/*.json`, and every new or changed string lands in all six files in the same commit (en, de, bn, zh, vi, es): same keys and `{placeholders}` as `en.json`; product, people, demo-company and connector names and trade acronyms (PO, WIP, T&A, ETA, QC) stay as written. The English fallback in `src/i18n/request.ts` merges top-level keys only, so it is a safety net, not a way to skip a language. Language choice (`src/i18n/request.ts`): the `merch_locale` cookie set by the picker wins, else the visitor's country from Vercel's `x-vercel-ip-country` header, else Accept-Language, else English; add a country to `BY_COUNTRY` when a locale is added. The picker (`components/lang-switch.tsx`) shows inline SVG flags, never flag emoji (Windows shows letters).
- The landing video has captions in all six locales: `web/public/video/via-merch-intro.<locale>.vtt`, generated by `web/scripts/build-captions.cjs` (cue times from a word-level transcript of the MP4). When the video or its voiceover changes, re-time and re-translate the cues and rebuild all six files in the same commit.
- Hosting is Vercel Hobby and Supabase free until Richard confirms the design; do not add Pro-only features.
- Functions and the middleware are pinned to `sin1` (`web/vercel.json`, `runtime: 'nodejs'` in `src/middleware.ts`) because the database is in ap-southeast-1. Keep page data loads in one `Promise.all`; `me()` is wrapped in React `cache()`.
- Models go through `src/lib/llm.ts` only: Claude Sonnet (`claude-sonnet-5-5`) for the agent loop and drafting, Haiku for extraction, DeepSeek for translation when its key is set. Every call records tokens and cost with `recordUsage()`.
- Agents: tools in `src/lib/agents/tools.ts` (service role, scoped to one tenant), the loop in `run.ts` (tool runner, logged to `agent_runs`), the inbound pipeline in `inbound.ts`. Inbound runs never get `request_approval`. Ledger actors are `Agent (for Person)` in chat and the agent name alone for inbound.
- Mail channels: Gmail (`lib/connectors/google.ts`) and Microsoft 365 (`lib/connectors/microsoft.ts`, Graph, Mail.Read and Mail.Send) share `lib/connectors/mail.ts` (tokens, known-sender filter, quote trimming) and `outbound.ts` (sending), with one OAuth flow at `api/connect/[provider]` (providers in `providers.ts`; migration 0008). Each member connects their own mailbox (gmail.readonly and gmail.send only on Google). Refresh tokens are sealed with `MERCH_TOKEN_KEY` (`lib/crypto.ts`) into `channels.auth_enc`, which only the service role can read; members cannot write `merch.channels`, so every channel change goes through server code with a role check and a ledger row. The minute drain reads new mail only (from the cursor set at connect: Gmail history id, Outlook receivedDateTime; never older) and only from known senders (contact emails and their non-free-mail domains) or one chosen label or folder; other mail is never fetched in full or stored. A reply goes out only from the mailbox the original came in on, after a person or the gate approved it, threaded under the original. Older mail is reached only through the agent tool `search_mailbox` (`lib/connectors/search.ts`): chat runs for a signed-in person, on that person's own connected mailboxes, bounded to known contacts and their domains, results not stored, every search a ledger row; bulk import of past mail is the historic-sync add-on. A catalogue entry is `stage1` only when its runtime works end to end.
- Google Drive and Sheets (`lib/connectors/drive.ts`, `components/drive-picker.tsx`, `app/[tenant]/drive-actions.ts`): a separate per-member connection (channel kind `sheets`, `config.connector` 'gdrive') holding only `drive.file`, connected through `/api/connect/google?want=drive` so it shares Google's redirect URI. VIA opens only files picked in Google's picker (needs `GOOGLE_PICKER_API_KEY` and `GOOGLE_PROJECT_NUMBER`); a picked file goes through the same code as an upload (`drive_file_id` in the orders import and the document reader forms; Google Sheets export as .xlsx, Google Docs as PDF, 4 MB cap). Disconnecting Drive deletes the token without revoking at Google, which would end the person's whole grant and cut Gmail too. Never widen to `drive.readonly` or `drive` without the restricted-scope review.
- Forwarding address and sending by hand (`lib/connectors/forward.ts`, `api/webhooks/resend-inbound`, `components/manual-send.tsx`): chats with no API for personal accounts (Zalo, WeChat, WhatsApp on a phone) come in when a member forwards them to their own `fw-<token>@MERCH_INBOUND_DOMAIN` (channel kind `email_inbound`, account = the address). Resend sends every received email of the account to every `email.received` endpoint, so the handler verifies the Svix signature, drops anything not addressed to a live forwarding address without fetching it, and accepts only mail from the member's sign-in email or connected mailboxes. The other apps on the same Resend account ignore mail to `in.getvia.xyz` before storing anything: via-crm `app/api/webhooks/resend-inbound/route.ts` and rrg `app/api/rrg/submit-email/route.ts` (8 Nov 2026). Any new app that subscribes to `email.received` on that account needs the same guard. Never use unofficial personal-account libraries for Zalo or WeChat. Approved replies with no connected channel stay approved, not sent: there is no hand-send or Mark as sent step (founder, 9 Nov 2026: removed until the WhatsApp and Zalo business-account connectors are live; do not rebuild it).
- WhatsApp Business and Zalo OA (`lib/connectors/{whatsapp,zalo}.ts`, `api/webhooks/{whatsapp,zalo}`; catalogue entries flagged `pilot` until proven with a live client): the client owns the account and VIA connects to it. WhatsApp (pilot, manual): an owner or manager enters the number's Phone number ID, a permanent system-user token and the Meta app secret (checked with Meta, sealed in `auth_enc`); the client's Meta app points its webhook at `/api/webhooks/whatsapp` with the per-channel verify token; every POST is verified with that channel's app secret (X-Hub-Signature-256). Zalo: VIA's one Zalo developer app (`ZALO_APP_ID`, `ZALO_APP_SECRET`, `ZALO_OA_SECRET_KEY`) is authorised by each client's OA admin through `/api/connect/zalo` (OAuth v4, PKCE, single-use refresh tokens kept by `channelToken`); events verified with X-ZEvent-Signature. These are company channels, so all inbound to the connected number or OA is stored, linked to a party by phone (WhatsApp) or `contacts.chat_ids.zalo`. Replies go out through `outbound.ts` after approval, inside each platform's free reply window; outside it the send fails with a clear reason and becomes a `send_failed` alert. Embedded Signup for WhatsApp needs VIA Labs to be a Meta Tech Provider.
- Alerts (`lib/alerts.ts`, migration 0014, `app/[tenant]/alert-actions.ts`): the safety net for what no connector captures. Hourly at :23 the drain raises `silence` (a factory, mill or customer milestone due within 3 days or up to 14 days overdue, nothing in from that party for 5 days and the milestone untouched; one per order and party) and `send_failed` (the `unsent` reminder was removed with Mark as sent); emails the person responsible once, escalates to owners and managers after 24 hours, and resolves each alert itself when the party writes, the milestone moves, the reply goes or the order closes. My day shows them with Chase (the agent queues a gated chaser) and Dismiss (a ledger row). Only the service role writes `merch.alerts`. The Connections page explains the three layers; keep it in step.
- Connectors are manifests in `src/lib/connectors/catalog.ts`; logos in `web/public/brands` (simple-icons SVGs or the vendor favicon); the Connections page renders the catalogue, so a new channel is a manifest entry.
- Customers and suppliers: country is stored as the ISO two-letter code (`planFromEtd` reads it) and shown through `components/country-select.tsx` with `lib/countries.ts`: names from `Intl`, flags from `public/flags` (flag-icons, MIT). `party-fields.tsx` is shared by Add, Edit and the document review. Tier is the supply-chain step: 1 garment factory, 2 fabric mill, 3 fibre or yarn. Edits go on the ledger.
- Archive and delete (`lib/party-lifecycle.ts`, migration 0013, `parties.archived_at`): owners and managers only. A party with no linked records is deleted at once; one with any is archived (hidden from lists, pickers, the agent's party list and ESG supplier tasks; its contacts no longer let mail in), restorable, and purged by the drain at :17 each hour 183 days later. The purge unlinks orders, messages, threads, drafts and milestones (they stay), switches off ESG requirements the supplier had to provide, and cascades contacts, bank accounts and its ESG tasks. New tables that reference `parties` must be added to `REFS` there.
- Add from a document (`parties/document`, `lib/documents.ts`): an uploaded proforma, invoice or PO (spreadsheet, PDF or photo, up to 4 MB) is read by `extract()`, and nothing is written until a person has checked the review form. The document is data, never instructions. The original file is not stored; the filename is on the ledger and the order's `meta.document`.
- Bank accounts (`merch.party_bank_accounts`, migration 0007, `lib/bank.ts`): a changed bank account is the classic invoice fraud, so every account starts pending, whether typed or read from a document, and only an owner or manager can confirm it, after checking outside the document, with the method and note recorded. The database enforces this: details cannot change after insert (a new account is a new row), no deletes, no reach for viewers, qc, compliance, logistics or VIA Labs support grants. Ledger rows and list pills show the masked number only. Agents have no tool that reads this table and must never be given one that sends or changes payment details.
- Legal pages: `/privacy` and `/terms` (`components/legal-page.tsx`) hold English text that governs, with the frame and the 'English applies' notice translated. Google's OAuth verification depends on `/privacy`: keep its Limited Use sentence word for word and keep every statement in step with the connectors, scopes, providers and retention the code actually has; change both together.
- The VIA wordmark is `web/public/via-logo.png` via `src/components/logo.tsx`; never type "VIA" as a text logo.
- ESG and compliance (founder, 7 Nov 2026; migrations 0009 and 0010, `lib/esg*.ts`, `app/[tenant]/esg`, `lib/agents/esg-pass.ts`; read `docs/compliance-coverage.md` and `docs/compliance-landscape.md`) is a paid add-on: `tenants.settings.addons.esg`, switched in the VIA console. One generic model covers every requirement a merch desk carries (bank covenants, codes of conduct, audits and CAPs, certificates, licences, chemicals, EU and US market rules): programmes hold requirements with a trigger (`schedule`, `per_order` at a milestone offset, `expiry` from the last accepted certificate), `applies_to` (`company` or each supplier matching `supplier_filter`) and an `order_filter`. Add a new kind of obligation as data (a template in `lib/esg-templates.ts` or a requirement), not as new tables. Tasks are unique on (requirement, key) and generated ahead by the hourly pass, which emails owners and queues gated supplier requests on the Compliance and CSR stream. Submissions are append-only versions with validity dates; evidence sits in the private bucket `merch-evidence`, served by signed link after a membership check. Reports are approved by an owner or manager only and are then fixed (`esg_report_guard`); nothing sends a report automatically. Template statuses are as researched on 7 Nov 2026; recheck regulations before relying on them. Template text is translated in `lib/esg-templates-i18n/<locale>.json` (same ids and requirement order as `esg-templates.ts`; a change to a template updates all five overlays in the same commit). Partners (role `partner`, migration 0011) are people at a customer or supplier who see and supply only the compliance records of their party: `requireTenant` sends them to `/app/[tenant]/shared` unless called with `{ partner: true }`, `my_tenants()` excludes them, and their writes go through `shared/actions.ts` under the service role after a scope check. Never give partners the assistant or any workspace page. Orders carry `destination` (ISO code) for market rules.

## Roadmap notes

- HK and China model profile (founder, 2 Nov 2026; Build Log Phase 10): Claude is not available to companies based in Hong Kong or mainland China, so they get a second profile on DeepSeek or Qwen behind the same harness, tools, gates, ledger and RLS. Plan: `tenants.model_profile` set in the VIA console and fixed by the harness; a provider adapter in `src/lib/llm.ts` mapping the tools and loop to OpenAI-compatible tool calling; a prompt per profile; one eval set run on both before any model change; usage per provider; `/trust` names the providers per profile. Keep every model call in `llm.ts` and the loop provider-neutral so this stays an adapter, not a fork. Required before the first HK or China customer; hosting for mainland data is settled per customer.
- Historic sync is a paid add-on (plan file, section "Add-on: historic sync"): import a customer's past POs, PLM approvals, QC reports, claims and price history under `source = 'historic'` so the agent cross-references them on new orders. Not in Stage 1; do not build it unprompted.

# Compliance landscape for garment, footwear and accessories merchandising

As of 7 November 2026. Research pass using web search only. Most results were law-firm, certifier and trade-press summaries, not the primary texts. Status column uses: in force, delayed, amended, repealed, pending, voluntary.

Confidence key: **V** = confirmed by two or more sources or one primary source; **S** = single or secondary source, treat as likely; **U** = could not verify in this pass (general knowledge only, re-check before relying on it).

## Read first: statuses most likely to be out of date in common knowledge

1. CSDDD was cut by Directive (EU) 2026/470 (Omnibus I): only companies with more than 5,000 employees and EUR 1.5bn turnover; transposition 26 July 2028, application 26 July 2029. (V)
2. LkSG: BAFA stopped receiving and checking annual reports; the government bill to delete the report duty and most fines had its first reading in January 2026. Final enactment not confirmed. (S, flag)
3. EUDR: still applies 30 December 2026 for large and medium operators (30 June 2027 micro and small). The Commission proposed dropping leather hides (HS 4101, 4104, 4107) via a delegated act; adoption not confirmed. (S, flag)
4. Bangladesh LDC graduation: the UN General Assembly moved it from 24 November 2026 to 24 November 2029. EU confirmed LDC preferences only "at least until end 2029". (S)
5. Textile DPP is not yet required: the ESPR textile delegated act is expected in 2027, with compliance realistically 2028 to 2029. The destruction ban on unsold apparel, accessories and footwear applies to large companies since 19 July 2026. (S)
